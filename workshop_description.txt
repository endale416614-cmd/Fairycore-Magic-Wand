[h1]Fairycore - Magic Wand[/h1]

Five small wands that shape a psycaster's focus into glowing orbs, each of its own element.

[h2]The wands[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Silver serpent wand[/b] (white): crackling orbs that pierce armor, burn with lightning and stun mechanoids.
[*][b]Elder wand[/b] (green): burns and poisons, 5% toxic buildup per hit.
[*][b]Vine wand[/b] (yellow): splashes the target with burning acid.
[*][b]Holly wand[/b] (red): sets the target alight, 5% heatstroke per hit.
[*][b]Ivory wand[/b] (blue): bites with frostbite, 5% hypothermia per hit.
[/list]
Every orb staggers what it hits, up to the size of a muffalo.

[h2]Psychic sensitivity[/h2]
The more psychically sensitive the caster, the fiercer the elements, up to three times as strong. The orb itself always hits the same.

[h2]Fire modes[/h2]
A button beside the wand switches between:
[list]
[*][b]Single shot[/b]: slow and steady, like a bolt-action rifle.
[*][b]Burst[/b]: three quick orbs, like a heavy SMG.
[/list]
The wand glows in its orb's colour as it is cast, and every orb bursts into light where it lands.

[h2]Psycasters only[/h2]
Only a psylinked pawn can channel a wand (Vanilla Psycasts Expanded psycasters count too). In anyone else's hands it is just a stick, about as good as a wooden mace.

A favourite of the pixies of Fairycore - Spirit Wings, but any psycaster can wield one.

[h2]Details[/h2]
[list]
[*]Medieval tech: researched with Smithing and made at a smithy from wood (wood and Eltex with Vanilla Psycasts Expanded).
[*]Not carried by NPCs; traders may stock them.
[*]Combat Extended: supported. No ammo needed: each orb costs 1% psyfocus instead. Without CE a wand never uses psyfocus.
[*]Royalty: needed in practice, since psylinks come from Royalty (or Vanilla Psycasts Expanded).
[*]Requires Harmony.
[*]Safe to add to an existing save.
[/list]
